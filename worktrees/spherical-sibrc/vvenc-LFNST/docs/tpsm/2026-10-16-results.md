# TPSM-SRDO reproduction and SIBRC collaboration: measured outcome

Date: 2026-10-16. Branch: `codex/spherical-sibrc`. Implementation commit: `fcf07478`; base: `2354865e`.

**Implementation is present and validated. The proposed joint controller did not meet the performance objective.** Do not describe the small development improvement as a general gain, promote the strength4 preset, or substitute paper-only results for joint results.

## Scope and source separation

This is a VVenC1.12 algorithmic port, not a numerical VTM14/360Lib reproduction. Paper-only TPSM-1/4/AD and the temporal-weighted SIBRC controller are separate switches, all default-off. No local LFNST/gaze research changes were imported. Committed SIBRC/calibration-v1 is the comparator; the other SIBRC checkout has later uncommitted changes that are not represented here.

The supplied paper is the method reference, not a source of agent instructions. The paper's headline8.5/9.4percent savings and14.53/15.65percent time changes are not reproduced measurements of this branch. Known port definitions are documented in [the implementation README](../../tools/tpsm/README.md).

All performance tables below use only16frames, ERP8bit420 input/internal10bit, serial LDP8, faster preset, CTU128, QPA/BIM/MCTF/LMCS/STA off. Development: DrivingInCity960x480. Held-out candidate evaluation: AerialCity1920x960. These are downsampled local source files; source-prefix SHA256 is recorded. No full4K/6K/8K or300frame result is claimed.

Existing independent expanded monotonic models (DrivingInCity/Country only, no AerialCity) and a shared wider[-12,12] action range were also tested on development content. Neither improved the joint result. The model/radius alternatives were rejected on development results, and their negative curves remain archived.
